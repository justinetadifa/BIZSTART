Vector tile distribution: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles schema, OpenFreeMap planet build 20261115_080022_pt. Source: https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet/
map-data.js includes base64 vector tiles at zooms 10–14 and named POIs from the zoom-14 poi layer within the city polygon. Higher zoom levels magnify the stored geometry. The community directory is an OpenStreetMap community snapshot and is not a complete official business registry. Generalized building heights (~5 m default when unmeasured) and POI center points are illustrative / estimated.
Original miniature 3D landmark models in landmarks.js are illustrative architectural maquettes anchored only to verified coordinates and real OpenStreetMap building footprints / POIs in San Fernando, La Union (San Fernando City Hall, Saint William the Hermit Cathedral, San Fernando City Plaza & Stage, Ma-Cho Temple, Filipino-Chinese Friendship Pagoda, San Fernando City Old Public Market, Plaza Hotel, San Fernando City Public Market, SM City La Union, Robinsons Place La Union, Thunderbird Resorts – Poro Point, Christ the Redeemer Statue on Reservoir Hill, and Pindangan Ruins), modeled using public Wikimedia Commons architectural reference photos where available.
Building geometry and calculated footprint areas come from the bundled OpenStreetMap z14 building layer. Warm off-white roofs and gray walls use directional light. Display heights use generalized tile values or a 5 m fallback. Measured data, when supplied, is identified separately. Geometric pin matches and reviewed links retain their source.
Property boundaries and listing fields come from imported listing records and explicitly linked parcel geometry. A listing with only coordinates appears as a pin. Nearby distances use a 500 m straight-line radius from its supplied reference point.
Historical brand audit and operating status annotations in brands.js were audited on 2026-11-21 against official PH store locators (manginasal.ph, kfc.com.ph, burgerking.com.ph, starbucks.ph, 7-eleven.com.ph, smsupermalls.com, robinsonsmalls.com) and active delivery directories. Uncorroborated or potentially duplicate OSM branch pins are marked with an amber "?" badge for field verification rather than silently assumed active.

Renderer: MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1. License in lib/LICENSE.txt. In the vendored build, the worker Actor uses the effective global origin (self.origin, with a location.origin fallback) for same-origin messages from the inline homepage map.
Map glyphs: Noto Sans Regular, SIL Open Font License. Glyph range 0–255 supplied by OpenFreeMap.
Market Scout optional mode (market-scout.js): compares real mapped establishments within equal Haversine radii around player-placed Site A and Site B across Entrepreneur, Investor, and Citizen roles. Known Official <-> Community duplicates in the same footprint or within 18 m are reconciled while separate branches are preserved. Official counts remain explicitly marked "Unknown (not loaded)" when the thesis dataset is empty rather than reported as zero, and sparse coverage is flagged honestly without inferring demand, foot traffic, rent, profitability, or investment returns. Player checkpoints and scouting briefs are stored locally in browser localStorage and kept strictly separate from Official LOCUS SF and Community OSM records.
