# LOCUS-SF use case evidence

Review date: 10 October 2026. Evidence is the current working tree, including pre-existing uncommitted changes. This was a focused static review of page entry points, navigation, authentication, API guards, payload policies and relevant services. “Implemented” means a reachable application path and its authorization were found; it does not mean a new end-to-end execution test was run.

The user subsequently instructed that the files in this workspace should be used as the reference. No separate attached manuscript was available. The scope reference is therefore the repository's `docs/system-manuscript-documentation.md`, supplemented by `docs/property-wizard.md`, `docs/automatic-assessment.md`, `docs/property-business-preview.md` and `docs/CLUP_GOVERNANCE.md`. ## Modeling decisions

- Broad discovery/assessment goals avoid turning fields, scores, pages or internal calculations into ovals.
- CAO/ASSESSOR and LEBDO are grouped because their checked property permissions match. The broker-review association carries an explicit authorization constraint; it does not grant review to every office user. In the property-only detail, all three offices share a City staff actor because their permissions in that scope match; office names are shown and no new persisted role is implied.
- CICTO's property-assessment association follows actual endpoint guards, not an assumption about organizational responsibilities. Broker review is never associated with CICTO.
- Figure 2 shows main role responsibilities rather than every shared public-page association. Brokers and city staff can also use discovery/comparison pages; this is an overview omission, not a denial of access.
- Use cases have solid actor associations. The detailed submission diagram uses a conditional extension for optional boundary plotting; its arrow points from plotting to the base submission/edit goal. No login includes, fabricated actor inheritance or process-sequence arrows are used.
- Scored reports, approved business-profile matches, official zoning/legal determinations and administration of assessment configuration are not claimed as unconditional implemented outcomes.
- Map/mail integrations are omitted from these human-goal views. Public news/weather/AI adapters and PRC are not given actor associations without a directly modeled goal. The internal database is not an actor.
