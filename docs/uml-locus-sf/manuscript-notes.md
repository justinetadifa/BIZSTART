# Figure explanation and discrepancies

## Caption

**Figure 2. Use Case Diagram of LOCUS-SF**

## Manuscript-ready explanation

Figure 2 presents the verified main user goals of LOCUS-SF. Guests can explore a limited public property preview, while investors can discover, compare and save properties. Brokers apply for authorization and submit or edit their own listings after meeting the account and credential requirements. Explicitly authorized CAO/ASSESSOR or LEBDO personnel review broker applications. CICTO administrators provision city accounts and manage reviewer permissions. Under the implemented permission checks, all three offices can maintain property records, inspect evidence and record due diligence. Assessment views retain pending results when required evidence or approved scoring rules are unavailable. Authentication and verification are preconditions for protected actions. The accompanying detail diagrams expand property submission/assessment and administration; optional boundary plotting extends property submission/editing and includes an estimated area display.

## Scope of the accompanying diagrams

- **Property Submission and Assessment:** verified broker listing preparation/editing, evidence submission, optional boundaries, assessment preview, city evidence inspection, due diligence and retained listing lifecycle. The City staff actor names CICTO, CAO/ASSESSOR and LEBDO because their permissions match within this scope. Brokers remain restricted to their own listings.
- **Broker Review and Administration:** broker application/reapplication, permission-qualified broker review, investor verification, city account provisioning, reviewer authorization, audit inspection, and public showcase/voting-option maintenance. Broker review includes document inspection and approve/reject/request-corrections/block outcomes; it is not assigned to CICTO.

Discovery is grouped in Figure 2 to keep the overview readable. It covers browsing/search/filter, map exploration, property details, preliminary assessment information, draft business suggestions and nearby establishments. Broker and staff access to shared public discovery/comparison goals is documented in the evidence table rather than repeating every public association in the overview. Saved properties remain investor-only. Secondary communication, site-visit, document-request and print/report goals are verified in the table but omitted for figure density.

## Unresolved discrepancies and excluded functionality

1. **Historical role descriptions:** `docs/system-manuscript-documentation.md:75` describes public admin creation and generic admin broker review. Current `api/staff.php:8` restricts provisioning to CICTO, and `app/Support/auth.php:573` restricts broker review to explicitly authorized CAO/ASSESSOR or LEBDO personnel. `seller-dashboard.php:105` and `api/_listing-policy.php:10` also contain stale text saying CICTO approves brokers. The figures use enforced permissions.
2. **Authority-to-Sell and listing-decision workflow:** `app/Support/PropertyAuthorityToSellFiles.php:41` and `:85` expose `id`, but `api/property.php:90` requires `fileId`. The normal review form adds `authority_to_sell_status` in `assets/js/admin-workspace.js:936`, so its approval/rejection request encounters that check. Reviewer metadata names also differ between the handler and normalization helper. This is a statically identified defect, not a newly executed production test. Document decisions and listing publication are outside the final diagrams. The guarded API-only listing-decision path remains present and CICTO-only; it does not resolve the normal UI blocker or broker-publication prerequisites.
3. **Scoring and governed configuration:** automatic assessment preview/readiness is reachable, but the repository ships without approved score conversions and complete verified source inputs (`docs/automatic-assessment.md:5`, `app/assessment-sources.php`). Approved business-specific profiles are absent and explicitly described as a proposed extension (`docs/property-business-preview.md:5`). CLUP dataset/rule/profile-review and immutable-report methods in `app/Repositories/ClupGovernanceRepository.php` have no reachable UI/API callers. Configuration administration, approved business-profile rankings and official clearance issuance are excluded. The existing draft exploratory business suggestions remain a distinct implemented discovery feature.
4. **Lifecycle documentation differs from code:** `docs/property-wizard.md` says restoring a deleted listing returns it to pending review. `app/Repositories/PropertyRepository.php:270` instead clears deletion metadata while retaining prior moderation, archive and availability state. Only unarchiving a historical `approval_state = archived` record resets moderation to pending review. The model follows code. Broker archive/unarchive is API-supported, but dedicated workspace controls were not verified.
5. **Manuscript reference identity:** no separate attached manuscript was available. After clarification, the user requested the files in this workspace as the reference. The comparison therefore uses repository documentation; it does not claim review of a separate thesis manuscript. Its old figure list assigns Figure 2 to an ER diagram; the requested use-case caption is supplied here, and the manuscript's figure numbering/list would need to reflect that choice.

The source of the groupings, permission conditions and implementation statuses is [the evidence table](evidence.md). No application functionality was changed.
